About

Background

I didn't take a straight path into process improvement. I started in data quality at RMSI, moved to Italy for a Master's in Design & Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, and worked in product design and graphic design along the way. Those experiences changed how I see problems — I stopped thinking only about whether something was technically correct and started thinking about whether it was clear.

At ZF Group, I was a mechanical design engineer — but what I kept being drawn to was the friction around the work. Drawings cycling through four reviews because tolerances weren't clear. Meetings where everyone had a different version of the same status. Documentation that existed but wasn't helping anyone. That's where I found my focus: not just solving problems, but making the way teams work clearer, more structured, and less repetitive.

That's what led me to Lean Six Sigma and continuous improvement. I'm an engineer who thinks a lot about clarity — how information moves between teams, where it breaks down, and how to fix it structurally. Technical depth plus design thinking plus process improvement mindset. That combination is what I bring.
